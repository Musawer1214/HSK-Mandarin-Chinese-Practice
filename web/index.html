Your weak words stay with you until they become familiar again.

Full decks show every word once in random order, including previously secure words. Misses are set aside for later. Focused practice begins with 4 warm-up cards, then targets weak and less-practised words. Missed words return after up to 3 cards; hesitant words after up to 5. Up to 40 attempts per round.

How a weak word becomes secure

Judge how well you recalled it before checking the answer. “Hesitated” and “Missed” add a word to your permanent weak list. Three confident recalls in separate rounds move it to Secure. Getting a repeated card right in the same round does not rush this process. Completed words are set aside in new rounds unless you choose to include them. The Practised bar counts words checked at least once. Recall progress gives each word 0–3 confident-round steps, so partial progress is visible. Secure still means 3/3; these are practice measures, not exam scores.
